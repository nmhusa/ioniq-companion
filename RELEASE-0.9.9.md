# 0.9.9 — Compact vehicle shortcuts

Vehicle check, Find my car and View surroundings share one row immediately below the vehicle image. All three use raised blue oval surfaces and wrapped labels, with a 56 dp minimum touch height. Vehicle check stays depressed when expanded; reported alert counts remain visible on its label and full status/freshness details appear below the row.

Lights, horn and location options remain available in Settings & help and by holding Find my car. Existing map, capture, authentication and command behavior is unchanged.

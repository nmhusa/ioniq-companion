# Car header asset

`app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/car_profile.png` is the transparent derivative of the car photo supplied by the project owner. Prepared using the built-in image-generation editing tool; the owner approved the resulting image. It is an RGBA image with actual transparent pixels. Battery percentage, charging state, lock status, range and odometer text are native Android overlays, not baked into the photo. Unknown values stay unknown, and the vehicle report timestamp remains visible below the header.

Editing prompt: “Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: supplied dark gray Hyundai IONIQ 6 photo. Remove only the white background, leaving a clean genuinely transparent alpha cutout for an Android app header. Preserve the exact car, wheels, badge, windows, proportions, color and front three-quarter viewing angle. Keep the entire car within frame with small transparent margins. Retain a subtle soft translucent contact shadow under the tires if possible. Do not add text, numbers, graphics, scenery or white areas outside the car. Wide landscape composition.”

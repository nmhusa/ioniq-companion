Privacy, plainly explained.

Applies to the source preview version 0.8.2.

The Android app

  • Uses your Hyundai account email, password and remote-service PIN to authenticate directly with Hyundai’s US API over HTTPS.
  • Stores credentials, session tokens and selected vehicle details encrypted in app-private storage with an Android Keystore key.
  • Encrypts vehicle status, selected vehicle model/year for the header, last reported odometer with its vehicle association and update timestamp, command results and saved parking/location data locally using Android Keystore. Temperature/UI preferences remain in ordinary app-private storage. Status and results can appear in widgets and notifications.
  • Contains no advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated proxy/server. Hyundai processes the requests under its own terms and privacy practices.
  • Optional Bluetooth auto-lock observes the selected car’s Bluetooth connection and stores its name/address, delay, opt-in, account/vehicle association and status encrypted on the phone. It does not use GPS or collect new phone parking coordinates. Once enabled it monitors using a foreground notification and can restart after reboot or app/system restart when Android permits. Pending countdowns exist only in memory and are not replayed after restart. A short wake lock supports the countdown and dispatch window. Turning it off cancels unsent work; commands already sent cannot be recalled. No automatic unlocking. The app does not request contacts, microphone or camera.
  • Find My Car requests the selected vehicle’s location from Hyundai when you request a location update or open Find My Car without a saved Hyundai location. It saves the returned coordinates, vehicle timestamp when supplied, retrieval time and vehicle association locally. Locations can be stale. Opening a map or walking directions shares the selected coordinates with Google via a Maps link; the map app may request its own phone-location permission. Clear saved in Find My Car removes both location caches. Account changes, vehicle selection and disconnect/erase also clear them.
  • Allows screenshots, screen recording and recent-app previews. Review captures before sharing them.

Optional unlock authentication

Unlock security uses Android’s system authentication prompt. The app does not receive or store your fingerprint or phone screen-lock PIN. This setting protects car-unlock commands; it does not lock access to the entire app. Screenshots, widgets and information shared with Maps are outside the encrypted cache.

Optional Wear OS companion

When enabled, the matching signed watch app exchanges command requests, command results and a small cached vehicle summary with the phone using Google Play services Wear OS Data Layer. This may use Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or Google cloud relay. No Bluelink password, service PIN, Hyundai client credential, access token, VIN or location coordinates are sent to the watch. The watch saves its last request ID, connection session and command result in private app storage to recover status without resending commands. The phone encrypts its watch session and duplicate-request receipts. Disable Watch controls to revoke access. Changing the selected account/vehicle also disables it. Phone unlock protection cannot be bypassed by the watch.

Removing data

“Disconnect and erase local data” removes saved credentials, vehicle status and command-result records. Parking settings and both saved location caches are also removed. Temperature and other UI preferences may remain. Uninstalling removes app-private data. This does not delete your Hyundai account or Hyundai’s server records.

# IONIQ Companion 0.8.6

The purple Settings & help bar stays depressed while its panel, including the caution/account area, is expanded. Tapping again collapses the panel and restores the raised appearance. The visual state now follows expansion rather than keyboard focus. All other behavior is unchanged. No watch update is required.

Odometer display now uses grouped whole miles/kilometers (for example, 32,325 mi), without a trailing decimal. Stored readings retain their original precision.
